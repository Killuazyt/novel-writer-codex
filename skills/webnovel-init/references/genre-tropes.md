# Genre prompts

Use these only as prompts when the author is stuck; they are not canon or mandatory templates.

- 都市：职业压力、身份差、资源门槛、可见的现实反馈。Avoid effortless status rewards.
- 玄幻/仙侠：力量代价、资源稀缺、势力阶梯、境界兑现。Avoid unexplained level jumps.
- 悬疑/规则怪谈：information boundaries, verifiable rules, costly tests, fair clue recovery. Avoid retroactive rules.
- 科幻：technology boundary, institutional consequence, scarcity, human tradeoff. Avoid jargon replacing causality.
- 历史：era constraints, logistics, institutions, and consequences. Separate known fact from fictional divergence.
- 游戏/电竞：rules, readable skill ceilings, team roles, and match-state reversals. Avoid arbitrary balance changes.
- 古言/现言/幻言：relationship agency, material constraints, social consequences, and non-romantic personal goals.
- 种田/年代：production chain, time cost, community negotiation, and incremental payoff.
- 快穿/衍生：world-entry contract, recurring core, differentiation, and copyright-safe transformation.

For a composite genre, choose one primary promise and one supporting mechanism. Do not combine two complete systems without a shared cost or conflict axis.
