# Character design

Build characters from desire, flaw, pressure, choice, and cost. The protagonist's flaw must cause a concrete bad decision; competence should solve one problem while creating another. Give major allies a personal goal independent of the protagonist. Design antagonists as pressure systems with a plausible value choice, not only stronger enemies.

Check that behavior follows known motives, relationships have bidirectional leverage, and every planned change has an on-page trigger. Names, labels, or backstory volume cannot substitute for decisions under pressure.
